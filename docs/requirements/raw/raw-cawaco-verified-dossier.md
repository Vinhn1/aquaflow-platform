## 2. Customer Identification & Invoicing Rules
- **Customer Code (Ma khach hang / Ma danh bo)**: Unique code printed on physical water receipts and e-invoices.
- **E-Invoice Lookup**:
  - Requires Customer Code + Verification code / password.
  - Invoice Form: 1/001, Symbol: K25TCN (Series 2025).
- **Consumption Recording (Ghi chi so nuoc)**:
  - CAWACO meter readers record readings monthly.
  - The existing mobile app allows customers to submit self-read meter indices online.

## 5. Administrative Customer Procedures
1. **New Water Meter Installation (Lap dat moi bo thuy luong ke)**:
   - Step 1: Submit application (online via portal or at physical counter).
   - Step 2: Site inspection and pipeline feasibility survey.
   - Step 3: Cost estimation & water supply contract signing.
   - Step 4: Physical pipeline construction and meter installation.
2. **Contract Transfer & Name Change (Sang ten hop dong cap nuoc)**.
3. **Meter Relocation & Height Adjustment (Di doi, nang ha bo thuy luong ke)**.
4. **Meter Accuracy Verification (Kiem dinh thuy luong ke)**.

## 6. Incident Reporting & Security Warnings
- **Incident Types**: Pipeline burst, underground leaks, low water pressure, turbid/dirty water, damaged meter seals.
- **Scam Warning**: CAWACO issued official warnings regarding fraudsters impersonating water staff via unofficial personal Zalo accounts requesting contract renewal fees or asking for banking OTPs.
- **Security Requirement for AquaFlow**: The Zalo Mini App must be authenticated, use official branding, and provide clear security verification to protect citizens against fraud.
